Press Kit — Avoken

Avoken is a structured practice app for iPhone and iPad built by Double Squeeze Productions, LLC. It offers short daily workouts built on established research paradigms — n-back, object tracking, go/no-go, task switching, and more — with difficulty levels calibrated to the published versions of those tasks.

The one-sentence version

Practice thinking. Daily.

What makes it different

  • Real paradigms, honestly implemented. Workouts follow the published task designs — planted trial quotas, lure items, bounded spans, switch-cost-aware timing — rather than borrowing names for generic mini-games.
  • No dark patterns. No ads, no streaks, no mascots, no engagement loops. Open, do a workout, leave.
  • No account, no cloud. All performance data stays on the device.
  • Honest claims. Avoken is a training app, not a treatment. We describe what each task asks; we do not claim the practice transfers to anything else.
