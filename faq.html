Avoken — Support FAQ

Can't find your answer? Email support@doublesqueezeproductions.com.

Subscriptions & billing

How does the free tier work? Three workouts per day, every day, free. No account, no card.

How does the trial work? New installs get 7 days of full access — every workout, every level. No payment method is required to start it; it ends on its own.

What does a subscription cost? $7.99/month or $39.99/year for unlimited access to the full catalog.

How do I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Avoken → Cancel. Cancelling keeps access through the paid period; deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com — developers can't issue them directly. Email us too and we'll help where we can.

I subscribed on another device / reinstalled — where's my access? Use Restore Purchases inside the app's subscription screen. Access is tied to your Apple ID, not the device.

Data & privacy

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in anywhere in the app.

Where does my data go? Nowhere. Performance history, progress, and settings are stored on your device. We don't run analytics servers or collect your data — see the Privacy Policy.

I deleted the app and lost my history — can you restore it? Unfortunately no — your history lives only on the device. Deleting the app deletes it permanently, and we hold no copy to restore.

Using the app

How are workouts chosen? Each day the app picks a short rotation that balances across the seven training areas, weighted toward areas you haven't practiced recently.

Can I pick my own workouts? Yes — the Workouts tab lists the full catalog; daily recommendations are suggestions, not a lock.

The app warned me about flashing/moving images — what's that? Some workouts use moving or rapidly changing visuals. A photosensitivity notice appears before those; motion-reduction settings in iOS are respected.

Is Avoken a medical or treatment product? No. It's a training app — the workouts implement published research paradigms, and we don't claim the practice transfers to health or everyday outcomes.

Bugs & feedback

Include your device model and iOS version, and screenshots if you have them — email support@doublesqueezeproductions.com.
